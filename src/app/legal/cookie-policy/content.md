## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners.

This Cookie Policy explains how Pitch Share ("we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies when you use our application.

---

## 2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for several important purposes:

### Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually set in response to actions made by you, such as logging in or filling in forms.

- **Authentication cookies:** Keep you logged in during your session
- **Security cookies:** Protect against cross-site request forgery and other security threats
- **Session cookies:** Remember your preferences and settings during your visit

### Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization. They may be set by us or by third parties whose services we use.

- **Preference cookies:** Remember your language, region, and display preferences
- **Theme cookies:** Store your dark/light mode preference
- **Layout cookies:** Remember your dashboard and interface customizations

### Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously.

- **Usage analytics:** Track which features are used most frequently
- **Performance monitoring:** Help us identify and fix technical issues
- **Error tracking:** Collect information about application errors to improve reliability

## 3. Types of Cookies We Use

### First-Party Cookies

These are cookies set directly by Pitch Share. We use them for essential functionality, user preferences, and basic analytics.

### Third-Party Cookies

We may also use cookies from trusted third-party services:

- **Supabase:** Authentication and database services
- **Analytics providers:** Such as Google Analytics, to understand user behavior and improve our service.
- **Error tracking services:** To monitor and fix application issues

---

## 4. Session vs. Persistent Cookies

### Session Cookies

These temporary cookies are deleted when you close your browser. We use them for:

- Maintaining your login session
- Remembering items in forms as you fill them out
- Ensuring secure navigation between pages

### Persistent Cookies

These cookies remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them. We use them for:

- Remembering your login status between visits
- Storing your preferences and settings
- Improving your experience on return visits

---

## 5. Local Storage and Similar Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use other browser storage technologies:

- **Local Storage:** Store larger amounts of data that persist between sessions
- **Session Storage:** Store temporary data that's cleared when the tab is closed
- **IndexedDB:** Store structured data for offline functionality

These technologies help us provide a better user experience by caching data and enabling offline functionality.

## 6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

### Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

- Block all cookies
- Block third-party cookies only
- Delete cookies when you exit the browser
- Get notified when a cookie is set

### Application Settings

Within Pitch Share, you can control certain cookie preferences:

- Analytics and tracking preferences in your account settings
- Theme and display preferences that use cookies
- Session duration preferences

---

## 7. Impact of Disabling Cookies

Please note that disabling cookies may affect your experience with Pitch Share:

- **Essential cookies:** Disabling these may prevent core functionality from working
- **Functional cookies:** You may lose personalized features and preferences
- **Analytics cookies:** This helps us improve the service but won't affect functionality

---

## 8. Cookie Retention

Different types of cookies are retained for different periods:

- **Session cookies:** Deleted when you close your browser
- **Authentication cookies:** Typically expire after 7-30 days
- **Preference cookies:** May persist for up to 1 year
- **Analytics cookies:** Usually expire after 2 years
